# BuilderConnect — Platform FAQ Knowledge Base

## About BuilderConnect
BuilderConnect is an online construction marketplace in Pakistan. It connects **Clients** (people who want construction or renovation work done), **Builders** (contractors who bid on and complete projects), and **Suppliers** (who sell construction materials). Payments for project work are made **directly** between the client and the builder against milestones — there is no escrow, and the platform charges **no commission or platform fee**. All prices are in Pakistani Rupees (PKR).

## Accounts & Getting Started
- **Sign up:** Click **Register** and choose your role — **Client**, **Builder**, or **Supplier**. Enter your name, email, and password, then submit.
- **Verify your email:** After registering you receive a verification email. Click the link in it to verify your address. You must verify before you can log in.
- **Log in:** Use your email and password on the **Login** page; you're taken to your role's dashboard.
- **Forgot password:** On the Login page click **Forgot password**, enter your email, and follow the reset link sent to you.
- **Roles:** Clients post projects and hire builders. Builders bid on projects and complete the work. Suppliers list materials for sale. (Support and admin accounts are created by the BuilderConnect team, not through public sign-up.)

## For Clients
### Create and publish a project
1. Go to your **Client Dashboard** and click **Create Project**.
2. Fill in the wizard: project type, property details, size (in **Marla**, **Kanal**, or **square feet**), location, your budget range, and requirements.
3. Save it, then **Publish** the project so builders can find it and bid.

### Review bids and award a builder
1. Open the project to see the **bids** builders have submitted (amount, proposal, timeline).
2. Compare them and click **Award** on the bid you want.
3. A **contract** is generated — review and **sign** it; the builder signs too.

### Milestones and payments
Work is divided into **milestones**. For each one:
1. The builder marks the milestone's work **complete**.
2. You **approve** it (or reject it with a reason if the work isn't right).
3. You **pay** the builder directly for that milestone and upload proof of payment.
4. The builder **confirms** they received the payment, and the next milestone begins.

When every milestone is completed and paid, the project is **complete** and you can leave a **review** for the builder.

### Find and compare builders
Use the **Builders** section in your dashboard to browse builders and filter by city, trade/specialization, rating, and experience, and to compare them side by side. Open a builder's profile to see their portfolio, reviews, and verification status.

### Cancel a project
You can cancel a project that is still a **draft**, **open**, or **in bidding** — and an **awarded** project before the contract is fully signed. Active bidders are notified and refunded. Once work is in progress, use a **dispute** instead of cancelling.

## For Builders
### Bidding and lead credits
- Browse open projects in the **Project Marketplace**.
- Submit a **bid** with your price, proposal, and timeline. **Each bid costs 1 lead credit.**
- **Lead credits come from your subscription — you cannot buy credits directly.** Choose or upgrade a subscription plan to receive more monthly credits. Your credit balance and history are shown on the **Subscription** page.
- If you withdraw a bid, that lead credit is refunded.

### Getting verified
Submit a **verification request** from your profile: add your credentials (such as your NTN or PEC number) and upload supporting documents. An admin reviews it and approves or rejects it, and you're notified of the result. A **verified** badge builds client trust.

### Your profile and analytics
Build out your **profile** and **portfolio** (past work, certifications, skills). The **Analytics** page shows your bids, win rate, earnings, and ratings over time — the trend charts are available on higher subscription plans.

## Materials Marketplace
- **Browse materials:** Clients and builders can browse suppliers' materials, filter and search, and open a product for details, stock, and reviews.
- **Place an order:** Orders are **Cash on Delivery (COD)** — you pay when the order is delivered. Provide your delivery address and contact details.
- **Track your order:** Follow the order status from confirmation through to delivery, and reorder past orders.
- **Reviews:** After an order is delivered you can review the product. Reviews from buyers who actually received a delivery show a **verified purchase** badge.
- **For suppliers:** Suppliers manage their catalog (add/edit products, stock, images), receive and confirm orders, arrange delivery, and see their revenue and stats.

## Help & Support
- **Get help:** Open **Help & Support** in your dashboard to create a support **ticket**. A support agent will respond, and you're notified of replies and when it's resolved.
- **Disputes:** If there's a problem on a project, the client or the awarded builder can **file a dispute** from the project. The other party is notified, support agents work it, and admins provide oversight.
- **Contact:** You can also reach the team using the support contact details shown in the site footer.
